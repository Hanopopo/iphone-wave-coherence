# Research notes

A working bibliography behind Wave Coherence. Every preset in the app links back to a paper or measurement on this page. Each entry is tagged with a **strength rating**:

- **STRONG** — replicated peer-reviewed evidence with measurable effect sizes.
- **MIXED** — peer-reviewed but with inconsistent or contested replications.
- **WEAK** — limited, single-study, in vitro / animal-only, or correlational.
- **NONE** — tradition or pop-culture claim with no peer-reviewed support.

The goal of the app is to make every signal honest: we emit real audio, light, vibration and 2.4 GHz radio. Whatever subjective or physiological effect a user notices comes from those measurable stimuli.

## Schumann resonances

The Schumann resonances are extremely-low-frequency (ELF) electromagnetic standing waves in the Earth-ionosphere cavity, excited by global lightning. The first mode sits around **7.83 Hz** (with modes at 14.3, 20.8, 27.3, 33.8 Hz, etc.). They are real, measurable, and have been continuously monitored since the 1960s.

### Cardioprotection from stress conditions by weak magnetic fields in the Schumann Resonance band — **STRONG (in vitro)**
> Elhalel, Price, et al. *Scientific Reports* 9, 1645 (2019). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-36341-z

Rat cardiac cell cultures exposed to a 7.8 Hz, 90 nT magnetic field show a 28 % reduction in spontaneous calcium-transient amplitude after 40 minutes, cessation of mechanical contractions after 30 minutes, and reduced creatine-kinase release under hypoxic and oxidative stress. Effect is frequency-specific (7.6–8 Hz only) and reversible.

### Similar spectral power densities within the Schumann Resonance and a large population of quantitative EEG profiles — **MIXED**
> Saroka, Vares, Persinger. *PLOS One* 11(1) (2016). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0146595

QEEG of 184 individuals shows a peak at 10.25 Hz; the first (7–8 Hz), second (13–14 Hz) and third (19–20 Hz) Schumann harmonics are discernible in averaged QEEG profiles in some participants. Real-time coherence between Schumann magnetic-field recordings (Sudbury, Canada and Cumiana, Italy) and local EEG within the 6–16 Hz band over microstate-length durations.

### Schumann resonances as a possible biophysical mechanism for human-health effects of solar activity — **MIXED**
> Cherry. *Natural Hazards* 26 (2002). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/a:1015637127504

Proposes Schumann-resonance amplitude as the mediating variable between solar-storm activity and human cardiovascular events. Provocative but largely correlational.

## Binaural beats and brainwave entrainment

When two pure tones of slightly different frequency are presented separately to each ear, the listener perceives a third "beat" tone at the difference frequency. If that difference falls in a brainwave band (1–30 Hz), the auditory pathway can entrain cortical oscillations.

### Efficacy of binaural auditory beats in cognition, anxiety, and pain perception: a meta-analysis — **STRONG**
> Garcia-Argibay, Santed, Reales. *Psychological Research* 83 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8

Twenty-two studies, 35 effect sizes. Overall medium, significant effect (Hedge's *g* = 0.45) on memory, attention, anxiety and analgesia. Effect depends on frequency, exposure time, and timing relative to the task.

### Binaural beats to entrain the brain? A systematic review — **MIXED**
> Engelbregt, Meijburg, Schulten, Pogossova, Konstantinovic. *PLOS One* (2023). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286023

Fourteen studies on binaural-beat-induced EEG changes. Five support the entrainment hypothesis; eight contradict; one mixed. Methodological heterogeneity is the dominant story.

## Heart electromagnetic field and coherence

### The energetic heart: bioelectromagnetic communication within and between people — **MIXED**
> McCraty, Atkinson, Tomasino. *HeartMath Institute* (chapter, peer-reviewed antecedents). https://heartmath.org/research/research-library/energetics/energetic-heart-bioelectromagnetic-communication-within-and-between-people

The heart's electric field (measured by ECG) is ~60× the EEG; the magnetic field, ~100× larger than the brain's, is detectable up to a few feet from the body with SQUID magnetometers. Inter-personal coupling between one person's ECG and another's EEG is reported during contact and at short distances.

### Heart rate variability biofeedback: how and why does it work? — **STRONG**
> Lehrer, Gevirtz. *Frontiers in Psychology* 5 (2014). https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00756

Resonant-frequency breathing (~0.1 Hz, i.e. ~6 breaths per minute) maximizes baroreflex gain and HRV amplitude. Used in Wave Coherence's "Heart coherence 0.1 Hz" preset (paced visual breathing).

## Cell-phone-emitted ELF magnetic fields

### Extremely low frequency magnetic fields emitted by cell phones — **STRONG**
> Misek, Jakus, et al. *Frontiers in Physics* 11 (2023). https://doi.org/10.3389/fphy.2023.1094921

Measured ELF magnetic fields directly in front and behind 15 different phones (2G + 3G, standby/speak/listen). Peak 70.03 µT (RMS) in the 5–200 Hz band on the front of a 2G phone during a call. Simulations show 6–8 µT in the midbrain during use. These are not the GHz cellular RF; they are very-low-frequency magnetic fields incidentally produced by the phone electronics — directly comparable to fields that have been investigated for biological effects.

The corollary for Wave Coherence: when the device is held to the head or close to the body, the phone's *own* circuitry is already producing measurable ELF magnetic fields. The magnetometer reading is influenced by this self-emission as well as by the environment.

## SSVEP (steady-state visual evoked potential)

When the visual field flickers at a stable frequency, the visual cortex produces a steady-state response at that same frequency, which can be read out from EEG. This is the basis for the Screen Flasher emitter.

### On the quantification of SSVEP frequency responses in human EEG in realistic BCI conditions — **STRONG**
> Wojcik, Klekowicz, Durka. *PLOS One* (2013). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0077536

SSVEP responses are strongest in the 12–18 Hz band, with secondary peaks around 10 Hz and 40–60 Hz. Used to choose the screen-flasher's effective frequency range.

## "God Helmet" / weak temporal-lobe magnetic stimulation

### Magnetic stimulation of the temporal cortex: a partial "God Helmet" replication study — **MIXED**
> Tinoco, Ortiz. *J. Consciousness Exploration & Research* 5(3) (2014). https://jcer.com/index.php/jcj/article/view/361

Twenty volunteers received 0.0001 T (10 mG / 1 µT) magnetic stimulation of the temporal lobes for 20 minutes; some reported "spiritual experiences" / sensed presences. Differences vs. controls significant on Whissel's Dictionary of Affect.

### Exceptional experiences following exposure to a sham "God Helmet" — **WEAK**
> Simmonds-Moore, Rice, O'Gwin, Hopkins. *Imagination, Cognition and Personality* 38(3) (2019). https://doi.org/10.1177/0276236617749185

Same exceptional experiences are reported when the helmet is **inactive**, suggesting the bulk of the effect is suggestibility / placebo / individual difference / time-of-day, not the magnetic field.

Net read: the magnetic field strengths an iPhone can produce (via the speaker coil, vibration motor, and onboard ELF emissions documented above) are roughly the same order of magnitude as the original "God Helmet" — but the dominant variable is suggestion. Wave Coherence does not claim spiritual effects.

## Cymatics

### Hans Jenny — Cymatics: The Structure and Dynamics of Waves and Vibrations (1967, 1972) — **MIXED**
> Original two-volume work. Continued in physics literature as Chladni-plate / Faraday-wave dynamics.

Sound waves produce ordered geometric patterns in sand, water and powders on a vibrating surface. This is real classical physics (resonant-mode shapes of a driven plate or fluid surface). The dashboard's animated rings are a stylized cymatic visualization of the current carrier.

## Solfeggio frequencies

### Influence of various intensities of 528 Hz sound-wave in production of testosterone in rat brain — **WEAK**
> Akimoto, Hu, Yamaguchi, Kobayashi. *Med. Hypotheses* / PubMed PMID 30414050 (2018). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30414050/

Rats exposed to 528 Hz / 100 dB show increased StAR and SF-1 expression, reduced P450 aromatase, increased brain testosterone, reduced ROS, and reduced anxiety-related behavior. Single rat study at extreme volume.

### Other Solfeggio frequencies (174, 285, 396, 417, 639, 741, 852, 963 Hz) — **NONE / pre-modern**

The Solfeggio set is a 20th-century reinterpretation of a medieval hymn syllable system. The specific "healing properties" assigned to each frequency in modern New Age literature do not have peer-reviewed replication. Wave Coherence ships them as available presets because users will look for them, and clearly labels them as tradition in the preset description.

## RNG / Global Consciousness Project

### Correlations of continuous random data with major world events — **WEAK**
> Nelson et al. *Global Consciousness Project*. https://noosphere.princeton.edu

Reports tiny statistical anomalies (~0.0001 effect sizes) in networked random number generators around major collective events. Method, statistics and pre-registration have been heavily contested; effect, if real, is far too small to be exploited by a smartphone app. Wave Coherence **does not** include an "intention RNG" feature for this reason.
